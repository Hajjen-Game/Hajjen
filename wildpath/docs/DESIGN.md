# Wildpath — design snapshot

**Decisions confirmed:** Adventure survival with a straight-forward, linear route; high distant orthographic camera; soft rounded fantasy nature and cozy colors (not blocky low-poly); intense Left 4 Dead-style pacing; a short high-score-driven run with boosts collected during play; two equal-score-potential classes (melee and caster); four combat actions per class (automatic basic plus three manually triggered specials); move while casting; hybrid targeting (click or Tab to select enemies; basic attacks auto-fire and spells hit the selected target); hidden crit chance; PC-first browser controls with mobile support planned. Fantasy tone may evoke classic MMO adventures but game terminology and assets remain original.

## Scoring principles

A stage has a **finite spawn budget** even when the Director adapts the pacing. Enemy type determines base points, and recent kills boost combo multipliers. Stage-completion rewards are equal for each class. Death stops a run; the score and personal best are displayed. No permanent stat buffs between runs: high scores should reflect skill, decisions, luck and score route rather than grind.

## Prototype departure from eventual target

The four-stage 0.1 playtest is deliberately shorter and uses procedural stand-in geometry. The target experience is roughly 8–12 minutes, with more polished enemy telegraphs, richer spell feedback and potentially imported licensed nature assets after review. Balance is preliminary. Class names Glintweaver and Stoneblade, skill labels and boost names are placeholders to evaluate the mechanics.

## Next design questions after playtest

1. Is the caster's spell targeting legible even without manual aim?
2. Does Stoneblade stay competitive against ranged enemies and swarms?
3. Does every Director relax phase feel earned, not empty?
4. Is basic attack cadence fast enough to be satisfying without being hectic?
5. Is moving through the trail more fun than stationary encounter gates, or should some gates open before every threat is defeated?
