# Willowglow — archived art experiment (not in playable Wildpath)

As of Wildpath v2.7.4 the Willowglow hero-tree experiments (original V11 and low-poly color study) have been disconnected from the playable game.

- No Willowglow mounts from src/main.js, even with old ?willowglow=1 URLs.
- No in-game style / palette / S-M-L experiment controls.
- Production trees and Nature Fusion continue to render unchanged.
- Original art studies remain in tree-study/ and src/willowglow-*.js for historical reference and possible future design work; these source assets are NOT imported by playable Wildpath.
- The Living Water environmental decoration has also been disconnected: no short-lived decorative wet patches or permanent lily ponds spawn and no pond HUD counter remains.
- Water-based attack names, short-lived combat spell effects and damage are retained (including Rainfall and Tidal Bloom).

**Playable game:** https://hajjen-game.github.io/Hajjen/wildpath/?rev=clean-world-274

This replaces the earlier Tree Readability 1.0 and Low-Poly Color Study in-game preview instructions. Their links should no longer be used to evaluate Willowglow in the game.
