COMBAT FEEDBACK

Dimmed abilities are currently unusable; range feedback becomes dim when the selected target is too far away.

The large center icon shows crowd control or an interrupt lockout affecting your character.

DIMINISHING RETURNS

Repeated PvP crowd control in the same category is reduced per target: 100% → 50% → IMMUNE.

Stun, Incapacitate, Disorient and Root each have separate DR. The category resets 20s after the last effect ends.

Unit frames show the next DR level and reset timer. Interrupt school-locks and the Warrior Charge mini-root do not use normal CC DR.

ACTION BAR & KEYS

KEYBINDINGS

Slots keep their keybinds when you move spells. Drag spells directly on the action bar to rearrange them. Click a key below, then press the new key. Ctrl, Shift and Alt can be combined with another key (for example Ctrl+W, Shift+R or Alt+1). Existing bindings are swapped automatically.
