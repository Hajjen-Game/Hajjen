# Gate of Echoes — Wind × Bamboo 1.1
1 November 2026. Level 54 / internal index 53.

Motivation: real player logs show little activity in the lower section of Temple Gate, alongside long objective droughts and technical shuffles. A fixed row-7 Wind may improve physical circulation without changing the Director or granting upgrades.

The level retains the original eight blocked cells, 46 moves and 7 Harmony Pulse hit objective. Every third valid move, a one-cell circular right shift affects row 7, with a persistent visible directional wind band. This is deterministic; invalid moves and fixed stones are unchanged. Genuine post-Wind matches resolve under the existing controlled Wind cascade rules.

The schedule is authored only for freshly started level 54 (including its unranked Bamboo preview). Older saved rounds without `windGarden` remain wind-free and retain the prior introduction; an explicit `windGarden:null` allows controlled A/B benchmarking. Other Bamboo levels are unchanged.

A dedicated node regression validates the initial seed, schedule, identities, old saves and exported wind events. A 390px mobile browser test validates visible forecast and actual three-turn movement. A seeded comparison records shuffle frequency, bottom-row activity, fusion destinations and goal stalls, distinguishing observed results from game-design expectations. This is an experiment, not a claim that a shift alone improves objective pacing.
